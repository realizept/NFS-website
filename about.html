Robert Blomquist is a doctor of physical therapy and the founder of Realize Physical Therapy, based in the Sandpoint, Idaho area. He is also the creator of the Neural Fascial Systems™ method — a proprioceptive-based framework for understanding and treating the body that he developed through years of clinical practice and advanced study.

Robert specializes in chronic pain and complex cases. His training includes functional mobilizations, visceral manipulation, and neural dynamics. He holds certification as a Functional Manual Therapist (CFMT).

In his free time, Robert enjoys tennis, sailing, paddleboarding, and hiking with his wife and kids in the beautiful North Idaho outdoors.

Neural Fascial Systems Courses

These courses were created to introduce fundamental concepts that transform the way you see your clients — and to progressively build toward advanced management of complex presentations. You can expect to enhance your understanding of soft tissue and neural connections while developing greater precision and effectiveness. The change is immediate and measurable.

Each course emphasizes improving observation and palpation skills while introducing techniques that reinforce those ideas. Applied anatomy and pathology content puts it all together so you can return to the clinic immediately and make a significant impact.

You will be introduced to the Neural Fascial Release Technique™ — a technique that combines established methods with intuitive palpation to identify restrictions that trace large lengths of the body. It requires minimal movement or pressure, focusing on releasing the entire restriction rather than a single point.

As you work through the seminars and lab courses, you will find content on anatomy, physiology, regional interactions, assessment tools, and communication strategies — all building toward a framework that significantly improves your clients' results.

Proprioceptive Principles

Precision Over Force

Realize Physical Therapy Clinic

In October 2025, Robert opened Realize Physical Therapy — a clinic specializing in the Neural Fascial Systems approach. Located in the Sandpoint, Idaho area, the clinic focuses on chronic pain and complex cases using the same principles taught in the courses.

The clinic and the seminars share a single mission: to give manual therapists a framework for seeing how the musculoskeletal, visceral, and neurovascular systems interact — so treatment addresses what's actually driving the problem, not just where it presents.

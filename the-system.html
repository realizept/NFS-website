Our Approach

The Neural Fascial Systems℠ Approach

A proprioceptive-based framework that transforms how manual therapists understand, assess, and treat the body.

A Different Way of Seeing the Body

We believe that tension on various tissues changes movement and impacts function. Restoring proper posture and enhancing muscle function through fascial mobilization yields better results than focusing on the muscle alone. By enhancing your assessment and treatment strategies, you can improve both the effectiveness and longevity of your interventions.

Knowing how soft tissue impacts muscle and nerve function helps you target the cause of tightness — not just the symptom. That means greater, longer-lasting results with your clients, with less intervention.

We encourage an eclectic approach. No matter your treatment style or specialty, the Neural Fascial Systems framework integrates with the modalities you already use.

The Neural Fascial Release Technique℠

Starting with the Neural Fascial Release Technique℠ (NFRT), you will improve your understanding of soft tissue connections and learn to quickly correct restrictions. Identifying the two ends of a restriction allows you to mobilize tissue with minimal effort and minimal patient movement — teaching the entire restriction, not just one point.

Core Principles

What Every Course Builds On

Fine-Tuned Touch

Developing precise palpation skills to find specific structures and restrictions — building the tactile sensitivity that makes treatment targeted and effective.

Visual Assessment

Identifying primary restrictions through observation before palpation — learning to read posture and movement patterns to locate the source of dysfunction.

Interconnected Systems

Visceral, neural, and musculoskeletal systems each influence one another — understanding these connections allows for treatment that reaches the root cause.

Three Body Systems

Understanding the Whole Body

The Neural Fascial Systems series approaches the body through three interconnected lenses. Each system series builds your ability to identify and treat restrictions that other approaches miss.

Musculoskeletal System

Fascia, posture, and proprioceptive assessment across the full body — the structural foundation.

Visceral System

Organ mobility and its profound influence on chronic pain, posture, and musculoskeletal function.

Neurovascular System

Nerve mobility, dural restrictions, cranial tissue, and vagus nerve function.

These systems do not operate in isolation. A restriction in the visceral system may present as chronic back pain. A nerve restriction in the upper quarter may cause persistent shoulder issues. The NFS approach teaches you to see and treat all three.

Built for Immediate Application

Eclectic by Design

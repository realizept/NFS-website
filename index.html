How We Teach

A Framework Built on Three Principles

Every course in the Neural Fascial Systems series reinforces these core skills.

Fine-Tuned Touch

Developing high-level palpation skills helps you identify the specific structure and restriction — so your treatment is precise and your results are consistent.

Visual Assessment

Learn to identify the location of primary restrictions through visual assessment before you ever touch your client. Get efficient from the first look.

Interconnected Systems

Visceral, neural, and musculoskeletal systems each influence one another. Understanding these interactions leads to longer-lasting results.

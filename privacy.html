Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Realize Health Seminars (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information collected through this website (neuralfascialsystems.com).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you use our contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

We do not collect payment information through this website. Course registration and payment are handled through our third-party booking system.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service. Submissions are transmitted securely to our email inbox. Please review Formspree’s privacy policy for details on how they handle submitted data.

Course registration is handled through a third-party booking platform. Please review that platform’s privacy policy at the time of registration.

4. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics software. Your browser may store session data for standard website functionality.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this website. No internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for licensed healthcare and manual therapy professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of this website after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

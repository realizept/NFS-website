Neurovascular System

Neurovascular System 1 Lab Course

Upper quarter nerve mobility, dural connections, cranial tissue, and vagus nerve — a 1.5-day intensive.

Important

Please note this course begins Friday evening and continues Saturday morning. Plan accordingly.

About This Course

This 1.5-day course provides an in-depth look at the neurovascular system through the Neural Fascial Systems™ approach. You will develop advanced techniques to improve palpation, assessment, and treatment — targeting specific tissues for meaningful results with less effort.

The course covers palpation and treatment of the primary nerves of the arm, cervical nerve roots, dural connections to the cranium, and the vagus nerve — using the Neural Fascial Release Technique™. Functional connections into other systems are woven throughout the curriculum so you can apply this knowledge immediately.

Anatomy and physiology of the nervous system throughout the upper quarter are presented to help you identify and address nerve-specific movement limitations. You'll learn how inflamed nerves impact symptoms and restrict motion — and how to restore that motion effectively. New techniques for targeting deep cranial tissue are also introduced.

What You'll Learn

  • Advance palpation skills for specific upper quarter nerves and associated structures
  • Palpate and address restrictions in the dura at the cranium
  • Enhance knowledge of nerve anatomy and physiology
  • Use proprioceptive input to release fascial tissue with the Neural Fascial Release Technique™
  • Understand how body systems interact to impact muscle function
  • Target treatment at the nervous system precisely for neuromuscular control effects
  • Improve manual treatment body mechanics
  • Identify problems stemming from the nervous system and localize them with precision
  • Apply other massage techniques to support neurovascular system health

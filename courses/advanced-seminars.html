Advanced Education Seminars

Monthly 1-hour deep-dives into specific conditions, clinical presentations, and advanced NFS application.

About These Seminars

Advanced Education Seminars break from structured learning models to teach principles beyond the standard curriculum. Each session is 1 hour and provides an opportunity to dive deeper into treatment strategies for specific conditions, clinical presentations, or advanced application of the Neural Fascial Systems™ approach.

Topics are participant-driven — you shape what gets covered. Each session is led by Robert, who provides clinical insight and context on each topic. Sessions build on skills that can be immediately applied in your practice.

These seminars are open to massage therapists, physical therapists, occupational therapists, and any manual therapy practitioner. Previous NFS courses are helpful but not required.

CEU Note

Not Eligible for CE Credit

Advanced Education Seminars do not carry syllabus-defined objectives and therefore cannot be submitted for continuing education credit under NCBTMB or Idaho state guidelines. Attendance documentation is provided upon request.

CEU Policy

All courses follow NCBTMB and Idaho state guidelines for determining credit hours. Content is consistent with Idaho state massage therapy license requirements.

NCBTMB Approved NCBTMB Approved courses are clearly noted on each course page. You will receive a certificate of completion at course end.
NCBTMB Pending NCBTMB Pending courses are not yet approved. You will receive documentation at course completion to support your submission to your licensing board — approval is not guaranteed.
Advanced Education Seminars Advanced Education Seminars do not qualify for CE credit, as they operate without a formal syllabus or defined objectives.

If you have specific questions about whether a course qualifies for your license renewal, please contact us before registering.

Course Policies

Continuing education credit, retake, and cancellation policies for all Realize Health Seminars courses.

CE Credit Policy

All of our courses follow NCBTMB and Idaho state guidelines for determining credit hours. Content is also consistent with Idaho state massage therapy license requirements. Courses that are certified will be noted, and for those that are not certified we will provide you with the necessary documentation to help you receive credit for each course you take.

Please note that while we do not certify within each state, some states (such as Idaho) will accept NCBTMB approved courses. If you have further questions please contact us.

Course Retake Policy

If you have already taken a course once, you may register a second time at a discounted price. We reserve at least 2 half-price slots per course.

Half-price registration does not include a manual or any additional supplies provided during the course. To register for a half-price slot, please contact us directly for availability.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

30+ days out

100% refund of the course fee, minus a $50 administration fee. This fee may be transferred to another course.

Under 30 days

50% refund available. Other circumstances must be approved in writing.

Any additional policies regarding special purchases such as bundles or products will be listed under the item in the online store.

Minimum Enrollment

We require at least 6 participants to hold a course (Advanced Education Seminars require a minimum of 2 participants). If enrollment is low 2 weeks before the course, it may be cancelled and you will receive a full refund. You will be notified as needed.

Questions about any of these policies? Contact us and we'll be happy to help.
